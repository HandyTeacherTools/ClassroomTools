How to Use the Classroom Timer

The Classroom Timer works in two modes: Countdown for timed activities and Stopwatch for open-ended tasks. In countdown mode, click one of the quick preset buttons (1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, 20, or 30 minutes) or enter a custom time using the minute and second inputs, then click Start. The circular progress ring shrinks as time runs out, and the colour shifts from green to yellow to orange to red as the deadline approaches — making urgency visible from across the room without a word being said. An audio alert sounds when time is up. In stopwatch mode, use the Lap button to record split times for activities like relay tasks or reading fluency checks. Press Space to start and pause from anywhere on the page.
