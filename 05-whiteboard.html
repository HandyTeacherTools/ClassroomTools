How to Use the Whiteboard

The Whiteboard gives you a full digital drawing surface, perfect for demonstrating concepts on the projector without needing a physical board. Select your tool from the toolbar at the top — Pen for freehand drawing, Marker for broad strokes, Line, Rectangle, Circle, or Arrow for shapes, and Text to type directly onto the canvas. Pick a colour from the palette or use the custom colour picker, and adjust the brush size with the slider. Switch between four background styles — dark, white, chalkboard green, or grid — to suit your lesson. Use Undo (Ctrl+Z) and Redo (Ctrl+Y) freely, and save your work as a PNG image at any time. Keyboard shortcuts are available for every tool — press P for pen, E for eraser, T for text, and so on.
