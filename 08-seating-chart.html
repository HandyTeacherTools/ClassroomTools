How to Use the Seating Chart Builder

The Seating Chart Builder lets you create and display a visual classroom layout with student names assigned to desks. Start by pasting your class list into the text area on the left — one student per line — then click Load Students. Choose a layout from the preset buttons: 4×4 Grid, 5×5 Grid, 6×4, U-Shape, or 3 Rows. Click Auto Fill to randomly assign students to desks, or drag names from the unassigned pool on the left onto individual desks manually. Swap students between desks by dragging one name onto another — they will switch places automatically. Drag a student back to the unassigned pool to remove them from a desk. Click Print to send the seating chart directly to your printer for a physical copy, or leave it on the projector so students can find their seats at the start of class.
