How to Use the Lesson Timer Pro

Lesson Timer Pro lets you plan your entire lesson as a sequence of timed segments, then run through them automatically on the projector. Start by loading a template — choose from Standard 60min, Double Period, Short 30min, or Exam Class — or build your own by typing a segment name, setting its duration in minutes, and clicking Add. Segments appear in the sidebar where you can edit their names and times. Click Start on the main display to begin. The large countdown shows the time remaining in the current segment, and a progress rail across the top shows how far through the lesson you are. When a segment ends, a sound plays and a full-screen transition card shows the name of the next segment — click anywhere to continue. Use Skip to jump ahead if a segment runs short, and Reset to start the lesson again from the beginning. Keyboard shortcut: press Space to start and pause.
