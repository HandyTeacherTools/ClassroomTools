How to Use the Flashcard Display

The Flashcard Display projects vocabulary, maths facts, or concept cards one at a time, making it perfect for whole-class review on the projector. Select a subject from the sidebar — Mathematics, Language Arts, or Science — then choose a specific deck from the list below. The current deck's first card appears on the main display. Click the card (or press Space) to flip it and reveal the answer. Use the Next and Prev buttons (or arrow keys) to move between cards. Click Shuffle to randomise the order. Enable Auto-advance to move to the next card automatically a few seconds after flipping — useful for fast-paced drills. Enable Shuffle deck to randomise on load. To use your own content, type your cards into the Custom Deck box in the format Question | Answer, one per line, then click Load Custom Deck.
