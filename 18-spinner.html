How to Use the Classroom Spinner

The Classroom Spinner is a fully customisable spinning wheel you can use for anything — picking topics, assigning roles, choosing activities, or adding an element of chance to any lesson. Load a preset from the sidebar — Yes/No, 4 Groups, Activities, 1–10, or A/B/C/D — or build your own by typing options into the input box and clicking Add. Options appear both on the wheel and in the sidebar list, where you can edit or delete them. Click Spin! (or press Space) to set the wheel spinning with a satisfying deceleration. The winning segment is highlighted and announced below the wheel with a sound effect. Click Remove Winner to take the selected option off the wheel — useful for elimination-style activities. Use Shuffle to randomise the order of segments, and change each option's colour by editing the wheel directly.
