How to Use the Word Wall

The Word Wall lets you build and display an interactive vocabulary board on the projector — far more flexible than a physical word wall because it can be updated instantly during a lesson. Type a word or term in the Word / Term field, add an optional definition, choose a card colour, and click Add to Wall. Words appear as colour-coded cards in the display area. For larger lists, use the Bulk Import panel — paste in one word per line, or use the format Word | Definition to include definitions. Click any card to zoom it full screen on the projector — ideal for drawing attention to a specific term during explanation. Click again (or press Escape) to return to the wall. Hover over a card and click the × to remove it. Use the A–Z button to sort the wall alphabetically, and Print to create a physical copy for students.
