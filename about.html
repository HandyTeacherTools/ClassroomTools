Why we built this

Teachers already have enough on their plates. The tools they need for the classroom shouldn't require a sign-up form, a subscription fee, or a 20-minute tutorial. They should just work.

We looked at the landscape of classroom tools and found something frustrating: the good ones cost money, the free ones require an account, and almost all of them store your class data on a remote server — raising questions schools understandably worry about.

So we built HandyTeacherTools.com from scratch with three non-negotiable rules: everything is free, no accounts ever, and no data leaves your browser. The tools run entirely in your browser window. When you close the tab, the data disappears. It's that simple.

🤝 Our Promise to Teachers

  • We will never charge for any tool on this site
  • We will never require you to create an account
  • We will never store student names or class data on our servers
  • We will never sell your data to third parties (we have none to sell)
  • The site will always be ad-supported, not teacher-supported

How we keep the lights on

HandyTeacherTools.com is funded entirely through Google AdSense advertising. When you visit the site, you'll see a small number of ads. Clicking on those ads (when relevant to you) is the best way to support the site and keep all tools free.

We deliberately limit the number of ads to keep the experience clean and distraction-free — especially important for a classroom environment. We will never use pop-up ads, auto-play video ads, or anything that would disrupt a lesson.
