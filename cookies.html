Our tools use no cookies. Advertising on this site, provided by Google AdSense, may use cookies to show relevant ads. This page explains how.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

Cookies We Use

HandyTeacherTools.com itself does not set any cookies. We do not use cookies for analytics, user tracking, or any site functionality.

Third-Party Cookies (Google AdSense)

We display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to:

— Show you personalised ads based on your browsing history across websites
— Measure ad performance and prevent fraud
— Improve ad relevance over time

Managing Cookies

You can control and/or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

— View and delete individual cookies
— Block all cookies from specific sites
— Block third-party cookies
— Clear all cookies when you close your browser

Note: blocking advertising cookies will not affect how our tools work, but may result in less relevant (non-personalised) ads being shown.

Opting Out of Personalised Ads

You can opt out of Google's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out via the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.
