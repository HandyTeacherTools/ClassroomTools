How to use

  1. Open a monitor and press Start listening. Allow the microphone when your browser asks.
  2. Click Calibrate to quiet room while the class is silent for 3 seconds.
  3. Use Fullscreen to project it, and adjust the Sensitive/Tolerant slider to suit the activity.

The microphone is only used to measure volume in your browser. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
