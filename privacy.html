Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

HandyTeacherTools.com is built on a simple promise: your data stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information entered into our tools.

1. Who We Are

HandyTeacherTools.com ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at handyteachertools.com, providing free browser-based classroom tools for teachers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Our tools are designed to run entirely in your browser. We do not collect:

  • Student names, class lists, or any data you enter into our tools
  • Voice or audio data from the Noise Meter tool (microphone access is local only)
  • Drawings or content created in the Whiteboard tool
  • Poll responses or voting data
  • Seating arrangements, point scores, or any classroom data

All data you enter into any tool exists only in your browser's memory and is permanently lost when you close or refresh the page. Nothing is sent to our servers.

3. Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may automatically log standard server data when you visit, including:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time of visit
  • Referring website

This information is collected by our hosting provider for security and operational purposes and is not used by us to identify individuals. Please refer to GitHub's privacy documentation for details.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

We display advertisements served by Google AdSense to fund the free operation of this website. Google AdSense may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to show you personalised advertisements based on your browsing behaviour.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

For more information on how Google uses data when you use our website, visit: How Google uses data from sites that use Google services.

5. Cookies

Our tools themselves do not set any cookies. However, Google AdSense and other third-party advertisers may set cookies on your device. These cookies are governed by the respective third party's privacy policy, not ours.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how advertisements are displayed but will not affect the functionality of our tools.

6. Children's Privacy

HandyTeacherTools.com is intended for use by teachers and adult educators, not directly by students. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

Our tools are designed so that student data (such as class lists) never leaves the teacher's device. Teachers should ensure they comply with their school's data protection policies when using any web-based tool.

7. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

8. Data Security

Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no database of user information that could be compromised. All tool data exists solely in your browser session.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under data protection laws (such as GDPR or CCPA) regarding personal data. Since we do not collect personal data, most of these rights are not applicable. However, you can always:

  • Opt out of Google personalised ads via Google's Ads Settings
  • Clear cookies through your browser settings
  • Use your browser's private/incognito mode

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
