Quiet Forest

Press Start listening

The microphone is only used to measure how loud the room is. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Classroom ideas
  • Project it, then click Calibrate to quiet room while the class works silently for 3 seconds.
  • Set a goal: keep the deer in the forest for the whole work period.
  • Choose the sensitivity to suit the activity: partner talk needs a more tolerant setting than independent reading.
