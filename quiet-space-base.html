Quiet Space Base

Press Start listening

The base only grows during quiet time, and it is saved in this browser so the class can keep building it across days. The microphone only measures volume. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Classroom ideas
  • Click Calibrate to quiet room while the class works silently for 3 seconds.
  • There are 10 modules to build: landing pad, solar array, habitat, greenhouse, comms tower, rover garage, lab, water tanks, radar dish and a big dome. When it is finished, a rocket brings the first crew.
  • On Mars, noise whips up a dust storm. On the Moon, it attracts meteors. You can switch planets at any time without losing progress.
