Sleepy Cat

Press Start listening

The microphone is only used to measure volume in your browser. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Classroom ideas
  • Click Calibrate to quiet room while the class works silently for 3 seconds.
  • There are 50 things in the room for the cat to wreck, from the coffee table to the bookcase and the pictures on the wall. A noisy room wakes the cat, and it keeps wrecking things until the room is quiet again.
  • The class can earn the room back: every 10 seconds of quiet while the cat sleeps, one thing is put back. You can also press Tidy up the room.
