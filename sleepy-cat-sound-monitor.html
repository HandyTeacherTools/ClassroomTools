Sleepy Cat

Press Start listening

The microphone is only used to measure volume in your browser. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Classroom ideas
  • Click Calibrate to quiet room while the class works silently for 3 seconds.
  • The cat stays asleep while the room is quiet. A noisy room wakes it, and it knocks things over until the room is quiet again.
  • A mess stays until the class earns it back: every 25 seconds of quiet while the cat sleeps, one thing is tidied up. You can also press Tidy up the room.
